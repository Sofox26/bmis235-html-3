Meet Lucita Fox!

Meet my mom, Lucita Fox! She is an important part of my family and someone who has made a big impact on my life.

On this page, you can learn more about her personality, interests, and what makes her special!

Her Athletic Background

My mom, Lucita Fox, has always had a passion for sports. Growing up, she played basketball and participated in track and field. Her talent and dedication eventually led her to Washington State University (WSU), where she ran track at the collegiate level.

I admire how she balanced athletics and academics. Her experience as a college athlete shows how hardworking, competitive, and determined she is.

Her Career as a Teacher

Today, my mom is a Spanish teacher at my high school! I think it is really special that she teaches at the same school I attend. She gets to share her knowledge of the Spanish language and culture with students every day.

Why She Inspires Me

My mom inspires me because she has been successful in both athletics and education. As someone who also loves basketball, I admire her competitive spirit and dedication to sports. She has shown me the importance of working hard, pursuing my passions, and always giving my best effort.
